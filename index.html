The Challenge

What Students Were Asked to Design

During Science and Technology lessons, students in Stage 2 were asked to take on the role of the designer. They were to pick an underutilized area of the playground at school and improve its design. The areas they chose to improve were the oval, the grass gap and the Sugarloaf COLA.

To complete their work, students followed the design process. They conducted research using Microsoft Forms to find out what students would like in a new space and discover what playground elements the school is missing. They took inspiration from natural designs and patterns to brainstorm ideas and themes for their new playground. They drew an annotated design of their playground plan before finally constructing a model. The model contains 3D printed parts which were selected and sliced by the students.

Your turn to decide. Browse the designs below, explore each one up close, and use your votes to show which designs excite you most. You can Like any design you admire, and Invest $100 (pretend money!) into any design you would genuinely back to be built.
