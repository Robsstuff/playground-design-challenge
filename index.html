The Challenge

What Students Were Asked to Design

Students were challenged to design a dream playground — a space that is safe, inclusive, exciting, and reflects the natural environment around them. Each design presents a unique vision: how children could play, imagine, and connect with their surroundings through thoughtfully crafted outdoor spaces.

Your turn to decide. Browse the twenty designs below, explore each one up close, and use your votes to show which designs excite you most. You can Like any design you admire, and Invest $100 (pretend money!) into any design you would genuinely back to be built.
